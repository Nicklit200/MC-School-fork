# Writing preference

Use simple punctuation in newly written user-facing text and generated story copy.
Do not use em dashes (U+2014) or en dashes (U+2013). Use a comma, colon,
period, or the ASCII hyphen instead. Apply this rule to story generation prompts
and template copy as well as explanations to the project owner.
Preserve verbatim source documents and transcripts; paraphrase when adapting them
for a story. Do not modify unrelated saved user content just to change punctuation.
